TeraBox Link Not Opening?

First check that the full link is copied and still active. Then try the official TeraBox site or app, another browser or network, and ask the link owner for a fresh URL if it has expired.

The link says expired, cancelled or deleted

Why it happens. Shared links are controlled by the person who uploaded the files. They can delete the folder, cancel the share, or TeraBox can disable it after a complaint or a rules check. This directory cannot restore a link that has been removed at the source.

What to try.

  • Open a different bundle. One broken link does not mean the others are affected.
  • Reload the page once. A temporary server error can look like a removed link.
  • If you need that folder, ask the original link owner to confirm sharing is still active and send a current link or code.
  • If the same link keeps failing, tell us which bundle number it is on the contact page so it can be corrected or taken down.

The link opens the wrong app or a blank page

Why it happens. Phones decide which app should handle a web address. Sometimes that decision conflicts with your browser, or an in-app browser (inside a social or messaging app) blocks the redirect.

What to try.

  1. Press and hold the bundle button, choose "copy link", and paste it into Chrome, Safari or another full browser.
  2. Open the TeraBox app first, sign in, and then open the link in the browser.
  3. If you opened this site from inside another app, use the menu to choose "open in browser".
  4. Check that the TeraBox app is updated to the newest version in your app store.

The folder loads slowly or looks empty

Why it happens. A bundle can contain hundreds of files and thumbnails, and each page of results has to be requested from TeraBox. A weak connection makes this much worse.

  • Switch from mobile data to Wi-Fi, or move closer to the router.
  • Wait a minute before reloading, then reopen the link once.
  • Close other apps that use bandwidth.
  • Use the app rather than the web page for big folders.
  • Clear the app or browser cache if the page never finishes loading.

Login or verification code problems

  • No email arrives. Check spam and promotions folders, confirm the address has no typo, wait several minutes and then request a new code. Requesting many codes in a row can delay delivery.
  • Code rejected. Codes expire quickly. Use the most recent message, not an older one.
  • Cannot sign in. Use the password-reset option inside the app. Do not enter your TeraBox password on any page other than the official app or website.
  • Account locked or limited. Contact TeraBox support. This directory has no access to accounts.

I cannot save files

  • Storage full. Open your account storage screen, delete items you do not need, and empty the recycle bin if the app has one.
  • Too many items at once. Select a smaller group, save it, and repeat.
  • Not signed in. Saving requires an account in most cases.
  • The save button is missing. The share may be restricted by the uploader, or the folder may not have fully loaded yet.

Video will not play or keeps buffering

  1. Update the TeraBox app, then restart your phone.
  2. Test your connection by loading another video site. If that is slow too, the problem is the network.
  3. Lower the playback quality in the player, if the option exists.
  4. Pause for a minute so the video can buffer, then resume.
  5. Save the file to your account or download it, then play it from there. This often works better than streaming from a share page.
  6. If only one file fails, it may be damaged or in an unusual format. Try another file from the same folder.

Downloads are slow

Free cloud accounts often have download speeds that are limited on purpose. If everything else on your connection is fast, the limit is probably on the service side. You can download in the evening or early morning when traffic is lower, keep the screen awake during large downloads, and download fewer files at a time. Paid options exist inside the app, but are never required to open links from this directory.

My browser shows a security warning

Stop and do not continue. A certificate or "deceptive site" warning means the browser cannot verify the page you reached. Close the tab, return to this website and use the button again. If the warning appears again for the same bundle, tell us on the contact page. Read the safety guide for what to check before trusting any login screen.

When to report a problem

Please report a link when it is permanently dead, when it leads somewhere that does not match its label, when a page asks for a password, payment or an extra app, or when it contains material that should not be shared. Include the bundle number, your device and browser, and a short description. Do not post passwords or personal documents in a public report.
