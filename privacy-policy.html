1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to terabox video link at teraboxlinks.pages.dev. It does not govern TeraBox, Google, GitHub or any other third-party website or application.

This website currently does not provide user accounts, payment processing, comment forms, newsletter forms or direct file uploads.

If you email the address shown on the Contact page, the site operator and your email provider will receive the email address and any name, message content or attachments you choose to send. Do not email passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive information.

3. Technical information

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare and associated infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information required to deliver and secure the website.

This may include:

  • IP address and approximate location;
  • browser, device and operating-system information;
  • request dates, times and requested URLs;
  • referring pages and diagnostic information;
  • security, abuse-prevention and server-log data.

Such processing is controlled by the relevant hosting and infrastructure providers under their own privacy terms.

4. Analytics and advertising

This website displays advertisements on the home page. They are supplied by the Adsterra advertising network and are loaded from the third-party domain deliberatewatchful.com. Advertisements are labelled “Advertisement” and are shown away from the bundle buttons.

To deliver and measure advertisements, the ad network and its partners may automatically receive your IP address, browser and device information, the page you are viewing and, where permitted, cookies or similar identifiers. They may use this information to show relevant advertisements, limit repetition, measure performance and prevent fraud.

The website itself does not load an analytics script. The ads.txt file identifies authorized advertising sellers but does not itself place cookies.

Advertising partners act under their own privacy policies. You can limit tracking by using your browser's privacy settings, blocking third-party cookies or using your device's advertising controls. If you do not want to see advertisements, you may use a content blocker.

Advertisers cannot change the content of this website's guides. If you see an advertisement that is misleading, unsafe or inappropriate, report it through the Contact page with a description of what you saw.
