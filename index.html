BEFORE YOU OPEN A LINK

Every bundle is a folder shared by an anonymous TeraBox user, and folders can change or disappear at any time. A few habits keep your account and device safe:

  1. Open links only inside the official TeraBox app or website, and never type your password on any other page.
  2. Do not install an extra app, APK or “player” that a page asks for. A video folder never needs one.
  3. Skip programs and unknown archives such as .exe, .apk, .zip and .rar files.
  4. Report dead, misleading or harmful links on the contact page so they can be removed.
