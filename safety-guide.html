Is a TeraBox Shared Link Safe?

A TeraBox URL alone cannot prove a file is safe. Use the official site or app, never enter your password on a third-party page, and avoid unknown apps or archives.

Why caution matters

Share links are created by anonymous users. Nobody checks a folder before it is shared, and a link that was harmless yesterday can point to different material today. Scammers also copy the look of cloud-storage pages to collect passwords or push unwanted software. A few habits remove most of the risk.

Red flags on a link page

Close the page if you notice any of the following:

  • It asks you to install an APK or any app other than TeraBox from your official store.
  • It asks for your TeraBox password outside the official app or website.
  • A "human verification" step sends you to surveys, prize pages or notification prompts.
  • It claims your phone is infected or your storage is full and tells you to tap a button.
  • There are several large green "Download" buttons that are not part of the file list. These are usually advertisements.
  • The page asks you to pay to unlock a folder or to "verify your age" with a payment card.
  • The address in your browser does not look like a TeraBox address, or it changes repeatedly through redirects.
Remember: opening a shared link never requires a payment to this website, and this directory never asks for your password.

Protecting your account

  • Use a unique password and a password manager.
  • Turn on any extra sign-in protection that the app offers.
  • Use a separate email address for accounts you do not fully trust. This limits damage if that address is exposed.
  • Sign out on shared or public devices.
  • Review your account's connected devices from time to time, and sign out of any that you do not recognise.

Checking files before opening

A video collection should contain video files and maybe a few images. Be suspicious of anything else.

TypeExamplesAdvice
Usually expected.mp4, .mkv, .mov, .jpg, .pngNormally safe to preview. Still check the size looks reasonable.
Be careful.zip, .rar, .7z, .pdfDo not open unless you know exactly what it is and trust the source.
Do not open.exe, .apk, .bat, .scr, .msi, .jarThese are programs. A video bundle has no reason to contain them.

Also watch for double extensions such as video.mp4.exe, and for a very small "video" that claims to be hours long.

Keeping your device safe

  • Install system and browser updates promptly.
  • Keep automatic app scanning (such as Google Play Protect) switched on.
  • Do not turn on "install from unknown sources" for convenience. If it is already on, turn it off.
  • Deny notification permission to websites you do not know. Notification spam is a common trick.
  • If something suspicious installed itself, uninstall it, change your important passwords from a clean device, and run a security scan.

Children and shared collections

Shared folders are not age-rated or moderated in advance. Children should not browse anonymous collections unsupervised. Parents can use device-level controls and app-store restrictions, and can sit with younger users when they explore any shared content.

Illegal or harmful material

If a link leads to material that is illegal, non-consensual, exploitative or involves minors, stop immediately. Do not download, save, forward or screenshot it. Report it to TeraBox through its reporting tools and to the appropriate authorities in your country, and tell us through the contact page so the link can be disabled. Our removal policy describes what happens next.

Quick checklist

  1. Official app from the official store only.
  2. Password only in the official app or website.
  3. Close pages that push other apps, payments or surveys.
  4. Do not open programs or unknown archives.
  5. Keep your device and apps updated.
  6. Report dead, misleading or harmful links.
