What is this website?

It is an independent directory of links to third-party TeraBox share pages, plus guides that explain how to use TeraBox. It does not host, upload or stream any files itself.

Is this site owned by or affiliated with TeraBox?

No. TeraBox names and logos belong to their owners. This directory has no official relationship with TeraBox.

Do I have to pay to open a bundle?

No. This website is free and never asks for payment. TeraBox itself offers paid plans, but they are optional and are bought inside the official app.

Do I need an account?

You can usually look at a shared folder without one, but you generally need a TeraBox account to save items. Downloading may also require signing in.

How much free storage does TeraBox give?

TeraBox has advertised up to 1,024 GB (1 TB) of free storage. The amount your account receives and the rules attached to it can change, so check the storage screen in the app.

Why does a link say it has expired or been deleted?

The folder belongs to the uploader. They can delete it, stop sharing it, or TeraBox can disable it. We cannot restore a link removed at the source. Please report dead links on the contact page.

Why is the folder empty or very slow to load?

Large folders contain many items that load in batches. Use Wi-Fi, wait a minute, reopen the link once, and try the app instead of the browser. The troubleshooting guide lists more fixes.

Does saving a file use my storage?

Saving an item to your account generally counts against your storage allowance. Previewing does not copy the file to your account.

Will saved files stay in my account forever?

Files you save are in your account, but nothing in a free cloud service is guaranteed. TeraBox can remove files that break its rules or change account terms, so keep a backup of anything important.

Is it safe to open these links?

The links go to TeraBox, but the folders are made by anonymous users. Follow the safety guide: use only the official app, never type your password on other pages, and do not open programs or unknown archives.

How do I report a broken, misleading or harmful link?

Use the contact page and include the bundle number and a short description. For removal from TeraBox itself, you must also use TeraBox's own reporting process.

How do I request removal of a link or file?

Read the copyright and removal policy, then submit the details through the contact page. Removing a link here does not delete the file from TeraBox.

Does this site collect my personal data?

The site has no accounts or forms and runs no analytics of its own. Its hosting provider, the advertising network that serves the labelled ads on the home page, and external destinations such as TeraBox may process technical data such as your IP address. The privacy and cookie policies explain this in detail.

Are there ads on this website?

Yes. A small number of clearly labelled advertisements appear on the home page, away from the bundle buttons. They come from a third-party ad network, help pay for the site and never change what a guide says. If an ad looks misleading or unsafe, please report it on the contact page.
