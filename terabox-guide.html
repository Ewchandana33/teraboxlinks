What TeraBox and a share link are

TeraBox is a cloud storage service. People upload files to their own account and can create a share link, a web address that lets anyone with the link view that file or folder without having to be given access one person at a time.

This website does not store any of those files. It is a directory: each button on the home page points to a share link that somebody else created on TeraBox. When you open one, you are looking at the uploader's folder on TeraBox's servers. What you can do from there (preview, save, download) is decided by TeraBox and by the uploader.

Good to know: because the files belong to other people, a link can stop working at any time. The uploader can delete the folder, or TeraBox can disable the link.

Before you start

  • A recent phone or computer. Update your operating system and browser so the app and the web player work properly.
  • Free space on your device. Downloaded videos can be large. Check your phone storage before you download anything.
  • An email address you can open, if TeraBox asks you to sign in. The service may send a verification code to it.
  • A stable connection. Wi-Fi is better than mobile data for large folders, and it avoids unexpected data charges.

Optional: Install the official app

Try opening the complete share link in your browser first. If you prefer the app or TeraBox asks you to switch, install it only from your device's official app store. On Android, the Google Play listing used by this site is com.dubox.drive; check the publisher and listing before installing.

  • Do not install an APK file that you found on a forum, a social post or a file-sharing page.
  • Do not install a second "helper", "unlocker" or "player" app that a web page tells you to get. TeraBox does not need one.
  • After installing, open the app once and allow only the permissions it actually needs. If you only want to view and save, it does not need access to your contacts.

Sign in or create an account only if prompted

  1. You may be able to view a shared folder without signing in. Saving or downloading usually requires a TeraBox account.
  2. If TeraBox asks you to sign in, open the official app or website and choose the sign-in or sign-up option. Available methods can differ by region and app version.
  3. Enter your email address and, if asked, the verification code sent to it. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive within a few minutes.
  4. Choose a strong password that you do not use anywhere else, and store it in a password manager.
  5. Check the storage amount shown in your account; offers and limits can vary by region and current terms.

Step 3: Open a shared link

  1. From the home page, tap a bundle button. It opens the TeraBox page in a new tab.
  2. If the app is installed and your phone allows app links, the page may open inside the app. Otherwise the browser shows a web version of the folder and may offer an "open in app" prompt.
  3. If the share page asks for an extraction code, enter the code supplied by the person who shared the link. This directory cannot recover a missing code.
  4. Sign in if you are asked to. Without an account you can usually look at a folder but not save it.
  5. Give a large folder time to load. Hundreds of items are listed in batches, so the page can look empty for a moment.
Link opens the wrong app or a blank page? Press and hold the bundle button, copy the link, and paste it into your browser's address bar. See the troubleshooting guide for more fixes.

Step 4: Preview, save or download

Once a folder is open you have three different things you can do. They are not the same, and choosing the right one saves time and storage.

ActionWhat it doesThings to consider
PreviewStreams the video from TeraBox without copying it anywhere.Needs a steady connection. Quality or speed may be limited on a free account.
Save to TeraBoxCopies the item into your own TeraBox account.Saved items generally count against your storage. They are yours to organise, although TeraBox can still remove files that break its rules.
DownloadStores a copy on your phone or computer.Uses device space. Free accounts may download more slowly than paid ones.

A sensible routine is to preview first, save only what you want to keep, and download only what you need offline.

Step 5: Work with large folders

A bundle can hold several hundred files, and trying to do everything at once is the most common cause of errors.

  • Save in batches. Select a few dozen items at a time instead of the whole folder.
  • Create folders in your account first. Naming them by topic or date makes the saved files easy to find later.
  • Sort the list. Sorting by name or date helps you see which items you have already handled.
  • Be patient with the first load. If the list stays blank for a long time, close the page and reopen the link.
  • Watch your storage figure. Saving an entire bundle can use a large part of your free allowance.

Managing your storage

  • Check the used and total storage in the app before saving a big folder.
  • Delete duplicates and files you will not watch again. Look for a recycle bin: deleted items may keep using space until the bin is emptied.
  • Download important files to a device or another backup if you cannot afford to lose them. Cloud accounts can be limited, suspended or closed.
  • Never share your login with other people. If you think someone else has your password, change it straight away.

Free account limits

Free cloud accounts usually come with trade-offs. With TeraBox these commonly include advertising inside the app, reduced download speed, and caps on things like single-file size or playback quality. The exact limits change over time and differ by region, so the in-app settings and TeraBox's own help pages are the only reliable source.

Paid plans are sold inside the official app. This website does not sell anything, and a web page that asks you to pay a "fee to unlock" a bundle is not part of TeraBox or of this directory.

Using shared files responsibly

Only save, download or share material that you are legally allowed to use. If you find a folder that contains illegal, non-consensual, exploitative or underage content, leave it, do not copy it, and report it to TeraBox and to us through the contact page.
